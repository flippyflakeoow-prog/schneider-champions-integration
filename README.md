---

## Official Press Release & Overview

**RUE MALMAISON, France & DUBAI, UAE** — October 14, 2026 — Schneider Electric, the digital automation and energy management leader, has officially finalized its acquisition of **Champions Aquariums LLC**, an engineering firm specializing in advanced aquatic infrastructure and automated life-support systems (LSS). 

The strategic purchase brings Champions Aquariums' proprietary fluid-dynamics telemetry and automated closed-loop filtration algorithms directly into Schneider's EcoStruxure industrial IoT framework. The move marks Schneider’s first major venture into specialized municipal and commercial aquatic automation, expanding its addressable market into large-scale aquaria, research marine laboratories, and high-density aquaculture facilities.

## Architecture Overview

This project provides the technical specifications and middleware required to integrate closed-loop aquatic life-support systems (LSS) into Schneider Electric's Modicon PLC hardware and cloud-based monitoring dashboards.
